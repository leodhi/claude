📖 How to Play

Each round
A Hebrew or Aramaic word from the deck is shown to everyone at once. Pick what you think it means from four choices.
Scoring
A correct guess earns +1 point. Wrong guesses don't cost you anything — just get the next one.
Winning
First player to reach the target score (set before the game starts) wins. The host can extend the game afterward if everyone wants a rematch.
Room Chat
Pass the phone around the table — classic pass-and-play.
