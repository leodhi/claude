Each player taps E / M / H to pick any mix of Easy, Medium, and Hard words for their own turn (Easy = little kids, Hard = older kids). Check "trace" for young artists who'd like a dot-to-dot outline when it's their turn to draw. Need at least 2 players. With exactly 2 players, both of you always score the same each round — there's no way for one of you to pull ahead. The game will end in a tie once you hit the target score, with the option to keep playing. Add a 3rd player for real head-to-head scoring.
