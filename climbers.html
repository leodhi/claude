How to Play

A note on this version
This is a browser adaptation of the physical board game "The Climbers" (Capstone Games). The mountain is a 7×7 grid of solid colored blocks instead of hand-stacked wooden pieces, rendered in 3D so you can see the actual heights. One simplification: blocks can be rotated to a new face (changing color/height) but not physically relocated, since this board has no empty space to move a block into.
The goal
Get your climber as high as possible. There's no finish line — the game ends once a full round passes with nobody climbing any higher. Whoever is then highest wins; a tie goes to whoever reached that height first.
Moving
Your climber can move sideways and/or upward across connected blocks that are your color or the shared gray neutral color — never downward, never diagonally. You can chain through several connected blocks in a single move if there's a legal path.
Ladders
You get one short ladder (bridges a gap of up to 2) and one long ladder (bridges a gap of 2, 3, or 4), each usable once for the whole game. You can use both in the same move if you land on a real block between them.
Your turn
A full turn is: climb → rotate one block (optional, can't be the block your predecessor just rotated) → climb again → place your disc (optional). Any step can be skipped.
Blocking disc
You have one disc for the whole game. Place it on any open block to seal it — nobody can stand on it, cross it, or rotate it. It returns to your hand automatically once play comes back around to your next full turn.
Bonus moves
After your full turn, every other player gets one bonus climb (no block or disc) in turn order, before the next player's full turn.
Room Chat
Everyone plays from the same screen, taking turns — the whole mountain is visible to all, so there's nothing to hide.
