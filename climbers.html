How to Play

A note on this version
This is a browser adaptation of the physical board game "The Climbers" (Capstone Games). Instead of stacking real 3D blocks, the mountain is a 7×7 grid where every square has a color and a height. The rules below describe exactly what this version does — a few numbers (ladder reach, board shape) were chosen to make it work well on screen.
The goal
Everyone starts at their own low, home-colored square at the edge of the grid. Be the highest climber left standing when the game ends.
Moving
On your turn you may move to one neighboring square (including diagonals) if it's your own color or the shared gray neutral color, nobody else is standing there, it isn't blocked, and it's no more than one level higher than where you are (you can also move sideways or to a lower square of the same height — never down to a lower one).
Ladders
Each player has one short and one long ladder, usable once each for the whole game. A ladder lets you leap further and higher than a normal step, as long as the landing square is still your color or neutral, open, and not blocked.
Rotating blocks
Instead of moving, you may rotate one unoccupied, unblocked square (that isn't someone's starting square) to its next face — this can change its color and height, opening or closing routes for everyone.
Blocking disk
You have one disk. Place it on any open square to seal it — nobody can stand there while it's down. It stays until play comes back around to you, then it automatically returns to your hand.
Your turn
Each turn you may do one climb (a normal move or a ladder), one rotate, and place your disk — in any order, any or none of them — then end your turn. If you truly have nothing you want to do, just pass.
Game end
Once every remaining player passes in a row without taking any action, the game ends and whoever is highest up wins (ties share the win).
Room Chat
Everyone plays from the same screen, taking turns — the whole mountain is visible to all, so there's nothing to hide.
