🐮 How to Play

Built for a child who can't read yet
Every round shows a set of pictures. All but one belong together — four fruits and a cow, say. Tap the one that doesn't fit. There are no words to read anywhere in the game itself.
Scoring
Get it right first tap and you earn a ⭐. Tap a wrong one and it just fades away — you keep going until you find it, so nobody ever gets stuck or knocked out.
Playing on lots of devices
Create a room, then share the link (or read out the 4-letter code). Everyone sees the same pictures at the same time on their own phone or tablet, and picks a character by tapping it — no typing.
Sound
The 🔊 button turns on sounds and a friendly voice that reads out what happened ("The cow! The others are all fruit."). It's worth having on for a young child.
