One player ranks each round, so at least 2 people are needed — there's no solo mode here.
A guess earns 1 point for every item that lands in its exact correct spot, out of 5 possible each round — so if you get 4 of the 5 spots right, that's 4 points; 2 right is 2 points, and so on.
The Ranker only scores if at least one guess gets all 5 exactly right — then the Ranker earns 5 points too. That's a flat bonus for the round, not per guesser: even if everyone nails it, the Ranker still only gets 5, never more. If nobody nails it, the Ranker gets 0.
