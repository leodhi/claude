🙊 How to Play

Setup
Everyone privately answers the same set of prompts about themselves. On one shared device the phone gets passed around; across devices everyone answers on their own phone at the same time.
Each round
One answer is shown with no name attached, and everyone guesses who wrote it.
Scoring
Everyone who guesses correctly gets +1 point. If nobody guesses correctly, the person who wrote it gets +1 point for stumping the table.
Winning
Every answer gets its own round. When they've all been played, whoever has the most points wins.
Room Chat
Pass the phone around the table — classic pass-and-play.
