🙊 Spill It!

Family trivia — about you. Pass one phone around the table.

🙊 How to Play

Setup
Everyone privately answers the same set of prompts, one at a time — the device gets passed around the table so only one person looks at a time.
Each round
The group reads one person's answer to a prompt out loud (no name attached) and everyone shouts a guess for who wrote it. Tap Reveal to find out who it really was.
Scoring
Everyone who guessed correctly gets +1 point. If nobody guesses correctly, the person who wrote it gets +1 point for stumping the table.
Winning
After every answer has had its round, whoever has the most points wins.
