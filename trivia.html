🧠 How to Play

Each round
Everyone answers the same trivia question privately, at the same time. Choose how you answer: type your own answer, pick from multiple choice, or a random mix of both each round.
Scoring
A correct answer earns +1 point.
Miss 2 questions in a row and you lose 1 point (never below 0). A correct answer resets your miss streak back to zero.
Winning
First player to reach the target score (set before the game starts) wins.
