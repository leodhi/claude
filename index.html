🦎 Chameleon

Play with friends on their own phones — everyone joins the same room

🦎 How to Play

The setup
Everyone but one secret Chameleon is shown the same secret word. The Chameleon only sees the category — not the word itself.
Each round
Every player (including the Chameleon) gives a one-word clue related to the secret word — vague enough not to give it away, but close enough to prove you know it. Then everyone votes on who they think the Chameleon is.
Scoring
If the vote catches the Chameleon (one player gets more votes than anyone else, and it's really them), the Chameleon gets one guess at the secret word:
• Guesses it right → Chameleon wins the round, +2 points.
• Guesses wrong → everyone else wins, +1 point each (not the Chameleon).
If the vote is split or lands on the wrong person, the Chameleon escapes detection and automatically wins the round for +2 points — no guess needed.
Winning
First player to reach the target score (set before the game starts) wins.
