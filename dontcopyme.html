📢 How to Play

The Judge
One player is the Judge each round (it rotates). The Judge sees the same category prompt as everyone else (like "an Italian restaurant") but doesn't compete that round — instead they secretly pick their own answer to try to trip everyone up.
Everyone else
The other players answer the prompt in secret, without seeing anyone else's answer or the Judge's. Playing on one device: pass it around — the Judge locks in their secret answer first, then each other player privately types theirs. Playing across devices: everyone — including the Judge — types on their own phone at the same time. Once everybody's locked in, all the answers reveal together. Answers are compared after trimming spacing, punctuation and capitalization, so "A Dog" and "a dog." count as the same answer.
Elimination
You're out for the round if your answer matches another competing player's answer, or if it matches the Judge's secret answer — both/all of you, not just one. Didn't lock in an answer in time? You're out too. Survivors get a fresh prompt (same Judge, new secret answer) and go again.
Winning the round
The Judge only gets 5 categories per round, and nobody scores until they're used up. If the Judge eliminates everyone, the Judge wins right away — no need to play out the rest. Otherwise, keep going through all 5 categories even if it's down to one survivor — they can still get caught matching the Judge later. If exactly one competitor is left standing after category 5, they win. If 2 or more are still standing, nobody scores — it's a wash. Either way, the Judge role passes to the next player and everyone's back in for the next round. First to reach the target score (set before the game starts) wins the game.
