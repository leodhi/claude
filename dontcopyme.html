📢 How to Play

Each turn
The app shows everyone the same prompt (like "an Italian restaurant" or "a scary movie"). Then everyone answers it in secret, without seeing anyone else's answer. Playing on one device: pass it around — each player privately types their answer, then hands it to the next player. Playing across devices: everyone types on their own phone at once. Either way, once everybody's locked in, all the answers are revealed together. Answers are compared after trimming spacing, punctuation and capitalization, so "A Dog" and "a dog." count as the same answer.
Elimination
Anyone whose answer matches another still-in player's answer is out for the round — both/all of them, not just one. Didn't lock in an answer in time? You're out too. Survivors get a fresh prompt and go again. If a prompt somehow eliminates everyone left, nobody's actually out — a new prompt is drawn and you try again.
Winning
The last player standing in a round wins it and earns 1 point. Everyone's back in for the next round. First to reach the target score (set before the game starts) wins the game.
Room Chat
Pass the device around — each player secretly types their own answer.
